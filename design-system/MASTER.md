# GCIT Project Examples — design system

UI/UX Pro Max was consulted with `architecture portfolio immersive minimal` (design-system) and `dynamic loading accessibility performance` (nextjs). Verified style match: Minimalism & Swiss Style; page pattern: hero-centric, immersive project experience.

## Direction

An editorial architectural portfolio, using warm off-white surfaces, grey/charcoal structure, restrained sage accents and strong whitespace. User preference for a white-and-grey house takes priority over the suggested orange accent. System sans-serif UI and Georgia editorial headings keep typography local, reliable and free of third-party requests.

## Interaction

3D loads only after a visitor chooses to explore or selects a room. Photographic model renders remain visible as posters. Separate exterior, ground-floor and upper-floor cutaway views; room buttons, an illustrative floor plan and previous/next controls navigate actual camera presets. Visible loading progress and retry feedback. Reduced-motion mode removes camera tweening and automatic rotation. Rotation is opt-in and pausable. Model rendering pauses while offscreen or hidden and otherwise renders on demand.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

Semantic headings and buttons; selected and expanded states announced; visible keyboard focus; descriptive camera controls alongside drag gestures; skip link; responsive room panel; no hover-only content. Check at 375px, 768px and 1440px. Do not introduce fictitious addresses, completion dates, client names or construction claims. The villa is labeled as a design concept.
